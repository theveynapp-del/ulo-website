FAQ

Common questions.

Who is Ulo for? +

Ulo is for women who are navigating a meaningful life stage — a new city, a new career chapter, a new relationship status, or a new identity. If you've ever walked into a room full of women and still felt alone, Ulo is for you.

How are matches made? +

We match by city, life stage, and career chapter. Every connection has a reason. We don't use algorithms that optimize for engagement — we use context that optimizes for real relationship.

What cities are launching first? +

Washington D.C. is our first city, launching May 2025. New York, Atlanta, Chicago, and Los Angeles are next. Join the waitlist to be notified when your city opens.

How is this different from LinkedIn or Bumble BFF? +

LinkedIn is for career transactions. Bumble BFF is casual. Ulo is for the woman who needs a peer — someone who understands what it costs to be where she is. We're not a feed. We're a membership.

What's the time commitment? +

Ulo is designed for women with full lives. A few messages a week, one Circles conversation, and one IRL event a month is the rhythm most members find meaningful. You control the pace.
